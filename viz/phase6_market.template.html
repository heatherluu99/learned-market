Research question

Metrics over time
How the simulation works
  1. Each week a buyer decides whether to shop at all — Poor 0.85, Middle 0.84, Rich 0.82. A buyer who stays home makes no decision, which is not the same as shopping and buying nothing.
  2. Stalls are noticed probabilistically. A stall's position_score sets the chance a given buyer sees it at all; unnoticed stalls are skipped entirely.
  3. Purchase is a random-utility draw. Price, remaining budget, and a fixed per-buyer taste feed a logit; a buyer keeps buying until the budget runs out.
  4. Memory accumulates. Returning to the same stall adds per consecutive week, capped so habit can match the strongest taste difference but never override it.
  5. Budget and stock reset weekly. Memory does not — including across weeks a buyer sits out.
